Store Policies

Clear terms for shipping, returns, privacy, and safe ordering with Taitva Pure by Nature.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

  • Serviceable Areas: We ship across serviceable Indian PIN codes directly from our facility in Rajkot, Gujarat.
  • Dispatch & Transit: Confirmed orders before 2:00 PM IST are processed the same business day. Estimated delivery is 3–6 business days depending on your location.
  • Order Tracking: Tracking details are shared directly on WhatsApp or email once your parcel is dispatched.

Returns, Refunds & Cancellation

  • Cancellations: Orders may be cancelled anytime before dispatch by contacting us on WhatsApp or email with your Order Reference ID.
  • Damaged / Leaked Parcels: Because juice bottles are consumable food items, opened products cannot be returned. However, if a bottle arrives damaged, broken, or leaking, share clear unboxing photos within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Resolution: Verified transit damage claims receive a free replacement bottle or a 100% refund to your original UPI/bank account within 5–7 business days.

Privacy Policy

  • Information Collected: Delivery details (name, mobile number, shipping address, PIN code) entered at checkout are used solely to fulfill and deliver your order.
  • Local Browser Storage: Your shopping cart, preferred coupon code, and personal order history are stored locally in your browser on your device so you can reorder easily.
  • No Data Selling: We never sell or share customer phone numbers or addresses with third-party marketers.
  • WhatsApp Updates: Promotional refill offers are sent on WhatsApp only if you opt in during checkout.

Terms & Conditions

  • Product Usage: Taitva Sea Buckthorn Juice is a natural food/wellness beverage. Please read the bottle label before use and consult a healthcare professional if you have allergies, are pregnant, or take regular medication.
  • Pricing: All prices are listed in Indian Rupees (INR).
  • UPI Verification: For UPI orders, dispatch begins once payment credit is confirmed against your Order Reference ID.
